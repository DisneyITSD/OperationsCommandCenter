🎯 Your targets for the month

Here’s what we’re aiming for each month, and what each number means for you.

  • AttendanceAim for 95% or higher
    The share of your scheduled days that you showed up. Being there on your scheduled days keeps the whole team’s day running smoothly.
  • Call and Ticket QualityAim for 95% or higher
    Your score from reviews of your calls and tickets. It shows how closely you’re following our quality standards.
  • FCR (First Contact Resolution)Aim for 82% or higher
    How often you fully solve the cast member’s issue on the first contact, with no follow-ups needed.
  • Work RateAim for 75% or higher
    How much of your logged-in time goes to actual work. About 25% of the day is already set aside for breaks and other activities.
  • Ticket ComplianceAim for 100% or higher
    Create a ticket for every call you take. Your tickets should match or beat your calls, so everything is documented.
  • Misroute RateAim for below 1.030%
    How often your tickets end up with the wrong team. The lower the better, so take a moment to triage each one right.

Small habits, steady results. Keep it up! 💪

ℹ️ How attendance is calculated
📊 Attendance Information
⏰ Actual log-in / log-out times are sourced from the CXOne (Agent Time Card) report. Entries submitted through the Late/Early Time Form take priority, and approved disputes override previous records.
📈 Attendance % = Hours Worked ÷ Hours Expected, using the same logic as the Performance → Attendance Tracker.
  • Hours Worked = the lower of (Shift Length − Late Minutes − Early Leave Minutes) and the total logged-in hours
  • Only time between the scheduled shift start and end counts; time logged before the start or after the end is not counted
  • Every minute late or left early reduces the hours; the 10-minute grace period only decides the Late / Left Early / Short Hours tag
  • Logged-out time during the shift of more than 10 minutes is tagged Short Hours
  • Called Off and NCNS count as expected hours with 0 hours worked
💡 Calendar colors indicate attendance status (Present, Late/Left Early, Called Off/NCNS, Leave, OFF, and Upcoming).
