                ℹ️ How attendance is calculated
                📊 Attendance Information
                ⏰ Actual log-in / log-out times are sourced from the CXOne (Agent Time Card) report. Entries submitted through the Late/Early Time Form take priority, and approved disputes override previous records.
                📈 Attendance % = Hours Worked ÷ Hours Expected, using the same logic as the Performance → Attendance Tracker.
                • Hours Worked = the lower of (Shift Length − Late Minutes − Early Leave Minutes) and the total logged-in hours
                • Only time between the scheduled shift start and end counts; time logged before the start or after the end is not counted
                • Every minute late or left early reduces the hours; the 10-minute grace period only decides the Late / Left Early / Short Hours tag
                • Logged-out time during the shift of more than 10 minutes is tagged Short Hours
                • Called Off and NCNS count as expected hours with 0 hours worked

                    ⚠️ Unexpected Agents

                      📝 Submit attendance records (called off, NCNS, running late, left early) directly from the dashboard.
                      They appear on Absenteeism and feed the Attendance tracker, Monthly view and Summary.

                      ⚠️ Important Reminder

                      Please review the Agent Name, Date (EST Shift Date), and Reason carefully before submitting. If you discover an error after submission, notify Manilyn so the attendance record can be corrected and dashboard reporting remains accurate.

                      Attendance data is automatically refreshed every 60 seconds. For real-time updates, go to the Dashboard page and click the blue ↻ Refresh Attendance button after submitting the form.

                      ⏱️ Submit the actual login or logout time for agents who were running late or left early.
                      It shows on Late / Left early and takes priority over the LOLI Actual time in the Attendance tracker, Monthly view and Summary.

                      ⚠️ Double-check before submitting

                      Enter the time in EST and check AM/PM. Use the shift start date as the date. Submitting again for the same agent and date replaces the earlier time.

                      🛠️ Dispute a late log-in or early log-out (e.g. computer / system issues).
                      Fill in Submitted by with the name of the person submitting, so the approver knows who raised it.
                      Once Approved (may take up to 24 hours to update), the disputed time replaces the actual time in Performance › Attendance tracker, Schedule & coverage › Monthly view and the Summary.

                      ⚠️ Before submitting

                      Use the shift start date (EST) and enter times in EST with the correct AM/PM. Fill in only the time you are disputing: the log-in time, the log-out time, or both. Always add a short reason and your name in Submitted by. Submitting again for the same agent and date replaces the earlier dispute. A dispute stays pending until it is approved.

                      🏖️ Submit PTO updates directly from the dashboard.
                      They appear on Leaves and count as leave in the attendance pages.

                      📋 PTO Reminder

                      Please verify the Agent Name, PTO Date (EST) and Reason before submitting. If corrections are needed after submission, notify Manilyn so the dashboard remains accurate.

                      📢 Submit dashboard announcements and reminders.
                      They appear on the Dashboard and the Announcements page.
